Help us help you

Please include as much of the following as you can:

  • Your friend code — shown on the Friends screen. This is the main way we can find your anonymous account.
  • Your display name in the game.
  • What happened — what you did, what you expected, and what you saw instead. Screenshots help a lot.
  • Your device and Android version (for example “Samsung Galaxy S24, Android 15”).
  • The game version — shown at the bottom of the Settings screen.

Account deletion

To delete your server-side account data, email us with the subject “Delete my account” and include your friend code. This covers data stored on our game server (Nakama), including profile and wallet information, friends, chat-related records, and moderation history, and, for players aged 13 and over, Firebase profile and leaderboard data where applicable. Local data on your device (settings, age-range profile, optional avatar photo, and local chat history) is removed when you uninstall the Game or clear app data, but server-side deletion requires a request from you. Verified deletion requests are completed within 30 days. See the Privacy Policy for details on what we process and store.
