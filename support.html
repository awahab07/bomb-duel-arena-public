Help us help you

Please include as much of the following as you can:

  • Your friend code — shown on the Friends screen. This is the main way we can find your anonymous account.
  • Your display name in the game.
  • What happened — what you did, what you expected, and what you saw instead. Screenshots help a lot.
  • Your device and Android version (for example “Samsung Galaxy S24, Android 15”).
  • The game version — shown at the bottom of the Settings screen.

Account deletion

You can delete your account from inside the Game: open Settings → Delete account and confirm. This removes your anonymous account, display name, friend code, profile photo, leaderboard entry, wallet, friends list and chat messages from our Google Firebase backend, and clears the Game's local data on your device. You can keep playing straight away — the Game starts you again as a new player.

If you cannot reach that screen, email us with the subject “Delete my account” and include your friend code. Verified requests are completed within 30 days.

Two things are deliberately kept, and neither can be linked back to you once your account is gone: reports you filed about other players, which are moderation records, and purchase receipts, which are bound to a one-way hash of your account and exist to stop a purchase being claimed twice. See the Privacy Policy for details on what we process and store.
