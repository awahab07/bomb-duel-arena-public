The arena bestiary

Some creatures guard the roads you know; others live in the wild stories bombers tell between matches. Hover or focus a card to reveal its legend.

Scorpion

The canyon gatekeeper hears a fuse through stone. It circles the treasure path, lets impatient heroes commit, then snaps shut the only safe lane.

Portal sentinel · Ambusher

Bat Bomber

A cave-born trickster that rides its own bomb through moonlit ruins. Every flutter is a feint; every dropped fuse lands where panic wants to run.

Night raider · Fuse rider

Bee Stinger

The gardens' furious patrol captain. Fast, territorial, and impossible to ignore, it turns flower-lined corridors into buzzing pursuit routes.

Garden hunter · Swift guard

Fire Wizard

Keeper of the factory's first flame. Its lantern reads the future in sparks and seals careless exits with walls of living heat.

Flame keeper · Lane controller

Ghost

A lost champion that never found its final portal. It drifts through silent halls, guarding the key it can no longer carry.

Portal echo · Relentless watcher

Demon Brute

The furnace made a monster from rage and iron. It does not solve mazes; it advances until the maze learns to move.

Furnace tyrant · Heavy pursuer

Dark Knight

Once sworn to protect the championship crown, this silent duelist now tests every challenger with shield, steel, and perfect patience.

Fallen champion · Armored guard

Mummy

Wrapped in maps from arenas erased from history. It follows old routes no living bomber remembers—and always arrives first.

Ancient pathfinder · Tomb keeper

Plant Monster

A hungry wall of roots and teeth awakened beneath the meadow. Break its cover and the whole garden seems to chase you.

Wild guardian · Close-range terror

Toxic Slime

Born where mystery energy leaked into the ruins. Slow to anger, impossible to predict, and always larger after a bad decision.

Mystery spawn · Living hazard
