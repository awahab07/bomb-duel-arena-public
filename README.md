Bomb Man Duel Arena is a landscape mobile action game built around quick
decisions, chain reactions, and dramatic last-second escapes. Cross guardian
worlds alone, fight real-time 1v1 online duels, invite friends into private
rooms, and build a personal collection of heroes and explosive effects.

## Enter the arena

- 🗺️ Journey through objective-based story worlds and guardian fights.
- ⚔️ Find online rivals or challenge friends in private real-time duels.
- 🔥 Master chain reactions, bomb kicks, shields, speed, and blast upgrades.
- 🎁 Risk Mystery Box rewards and survive sudden-death arena collapse.
- 🎨 Collect heroes, bomb skins, explosion effects, and avatar frames.
- 💬 Add friends, send invites, and choose Everyone or Friends Only lobby chat.
